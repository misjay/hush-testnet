Questions people ask first

Do I really not need an account?

Really. Hush opens a vault for you with a secret code. Whoever holds the code controls the vault, so keep it safe. Hush can't recover a lost one.

Why is my balance in dollars?

Your testnet ZEC is valued at the live ZEC price when it arrives, so bets feel like the real thing. When you withdraw, Hush sends back testnet ZEC at the price at that moment.

Where do the markets come from?

Most are mirrored from Polymarket, including prices and how they resolve. Zcash-native markets are run by Hush and settled from public chain data.

Is any of this real money?

No. Hush is running on Zcash testnet, so every coin is a free testnet coin with no value. The markets, prices and results are real, copied from Polymarket.

From faucet to first bet

About ten minutes, most of it waiting for the testnet to confirm.

  1. 1

    Get a testnet wallet address

    You need a Zcash wallet that can run on testnet. Testnet addresses start with utest1 (unified) or ztestsapling1 (shielded). If your wallet shows u1 or zs1, it's on mainnet. Switch it to testnet first.

    Developers can use zingo-cli with --chain testnet.

  2. 2

    Ask a faucet for TAZ

    Paste your testnet address into a faucet. Each opens in a new tab. If one is empty or down, try the other.

  3. 3

    Send it to your Hush vault

    Open your vault, tap Deposit and send your TAZ to the shielded address shown there. Your vault is credited in test dollars at the live ZEC price once it confirms.

Both faucets are empty. What now?

Testnet faucets are run by volunteers and sometimes run dry. Ask for a few TAZ in the Zcash Community Forum; people there regularly send testnet coins to builders. Your $250 practice balance works in the meantime.

Can I send real ZEC to Hush testnet?

No. Never send mainnet ZEC to a testnet address. It won't arrive and can't be recovered. Hush testnet only works with TAZ.

Are testnet coins worth anything?

No. TAZ exists only for testing. That's why you can learn private betting on Hush with nothing at stake.

How long does it take?

Faucets usually send within a few minutes. A deposit to Hush shows up after a few testnet confirmations, often under ten minutes.
